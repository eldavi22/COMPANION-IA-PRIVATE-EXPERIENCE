## 🧠 Engineering Approach

My development approach is based on the following principles:

1. Understand the business and technical problem.
2. Define the functional and non-functional requirements.
3. Select technologies according to the project's needs.
4. Design a clear and modular architecture.
5. Implement, test, and iterate.
6. Consider security, maintainability, and operational risks.
7. Deliver a working solution and continuously improve it.

I believe that a strong engineer should be able to adapt to different environments while maintaining technical discipline and understanding the broader business context.
